Strategic Value Proposition

Five Pillars of National Healthcare Modernization

Engineered to solve critical bottlenecks in public health delivery, optimize donor grant allocations, and ensure 100% data sovereignty under national jurisdiction.

🆔

1. Universal Citizen Health Registry (EHR)

Eliminates duplicate paper records across regional health facilities with normalized longitudinal citizen records, linking diagnoses, lab results, prescriptions, and NHIMA insurance coverage.

🏥

2. Nationwide Bed & Emergency Grid

Live real-time visibility into ICU, Emergency, Operating Theatre, and General Ward bed occupancy to streamline patient transfers and national outbreak disaster preparedness.

💊

3. Essential Medicines Stockout Prevention

Tracks medication inventory, lot numbers, and dispensing against doctor prescriptions in real-time, preventing pharmaceutical leakages and stock exhaustion in public pharmacies.

💰

4. NHIMA Interoperability & Revenue Cycle

Automates public health insurance claim validation, patient co-payments, and subsidized fee waivers to ensure transparent fiscal governance and zero revenue leakage.

🛡️

5. Sovereign DBA Security & Resilience

Powered by a 3NF PostgreSQL architecture with primary-to-replica streaming replication, sub-15-minute RPO/RTO disaster recovery, and 4-tier least-privilege RBAC.

📈

6. Epidemiological Disease Surveillance

Aggregated diagnostic data feeds directly into national public health dashboards, enabling rapid detection of disease outbreaks and targeted immunization planning.
