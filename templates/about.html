1. Executive Summary & Policy Alignment

Public healthcare delivery across the nation faces three systemic challenges: fragmented patient records leading to repeated diagnostics, uncoordinated bed capacity causing emergency triage delays, and manual pharmaceutical tracking resulting in stockouts and revenue leakage.

AegisHealth National provides an end-to-end sovereign digital platform. It unifies citizen Electronic Health Records (EHR), real-time bed management (ICU, Emergency, Operating, General), doctor clinical workflows, pharmacy inventory tracking, and National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIMA) claims settlement on top of an auditable, high-performance PostgreSQL 3NF relational database.

2. Critical National Problems & Delivered Solutions

⚠️ Existing Public Health Bottlenecks

  • Paper records degrade, get lost during patient facility transfers, and prevent longitudinal care tracking.
  • Critical care ambulances arrive at regional hospitals with no prior visibility of ICU or ventilator bed availability.
  • Essential medicines stockouts occur due to unmonitored dispensing and disconnected pharmacy registries.
  • Public hospital billing processes leak funds due to delayed NHIMA insurance verification and untracked fee waivers.

✅ AegisHealth National Interventions

  • Single Source of Truth: Centralized 3NF schema links patient identity, blood type, clinical history, and prescriptions.
  • Live Bed Grid: Instant ward status (ICU, Emergency, General) prevents critical care transfer bottlenecks.
  • Inventory Control: Direct binding of medication stock decrements to doctor clinical prescriptions.
  • Transparent Revenue: Direct NHIMA policy mapping and transparent billing status audit trail.

4. Enterprise Database Administration & Security Architecture

Built with enterprise-grade relational database rigor to guarantee zero data loss, high concurrency, and strict privacy protection under the National Data Protection Act.

🔒 3NF Normalized Schema

Every entity (`patients`, `doctors`, `departments`, `rooms`, `admissions`, `prescriptions`, `billing`) adheres to Third Normal Form to prevent update anomalies and guarantee relational integrity.

🔄 Streaming Replication

Real-time PostgreSQL streaming replication (`db-primary` → `db-replica`) allows clinical staff to read/write seamlessly while analytics and reporting tools run on the replica.

🛡️ 4-Tier Least-Privilege RBAC

Four isolated roles (`hospital_admin`, `hospital_doctor`, `hospital_reception`, `hospital_analyst`) ensure reception cannot view clinical notes, while doctors cannot modify fiscal ledgers.
